Task 3: RAID-4 Block-Level Striping & XOR Parity Recovery Engine
P = D1 ⊕ D2 ⊕ D3 ⊕ D4 ⊕ D5 ⊕ D6

Task-3 Specification: RAID-4 stores data across 6 Data Drives (D1 to D6) with 1 Dedicated Parity Drive (P). Data Blocks: D1 = 1010, D2 = 1100, D3 = 0111, D4 = 1001, D5 = 0011, D6 = 1110. Parity equation yields Parity P = 0101. If Drive D5 crashes, its content is reconstructed via bitwise XOR: D5 = D1 ⊕ D2 ⊕ D3 ⊕ D4 ⊕ D6 ⊕ P.
